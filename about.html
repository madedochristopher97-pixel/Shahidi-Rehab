About Shahidi Centre

Standing as Witnesses to Human Renewal

In Swahili, Shahidi means "witness." We exist to walk alongside individuals and families through their most vulnerable hours—witnessing their courage, restoring their health, and celebrating their lasting transformation.

Our Founding Purpose

Why Shahidi Centre Was Born

Shahidi Centre for Recovery was established by a multidisciplinary circle of addiction psychiatrists, clinical psychologists, and family advocates who saw a profound gap in East African addiction treatment: too many facilities relied on punitive, confrontational methods, institutional coldness, or superficial short-term fixes.

We believed there was a better, more humane way. We envisioned a medical sanctuary that combines the highest standards of evidence-based neuropsychiatry with genuine African hospitality, trauma-informed empathy, and serene natural beauty.

Recovery is neither a moral failure nor a test of willpower. It is an intricate neurobiological and emotional journey that requires compassionate medical stabilization, deep psychological inquiry, and a safe community where vulnerability is met with respect.

Our Sacred Covenant Zero stigma, absolute confidentiality, and unconditional positive regard
Clinical Framework

Our Four Cornerstones of Sustainable Recovery

As a newly launched, state-of-the-art centre, our clinical model is built upon internationally validated addiction medicine protocols tailored thoughtfully to our cultural context.

1. Medical & Neurobiological Care

Addiction alters brain chemistry and autonomic regulation. Our medical team uses evidence-based pharmacotherapies to alleviate withdrawal safely and restore healthy sleep architecture, neurotransmitter balance, and nutritional vitality.

2. Trauma-Informed Psychotherapy

We look beneath the behavior to uncover the unresolved grief, developmental trauma, chronic stress, or co-occurring mood disorders that often fuel addictive coping mechanisms. Our clinicians utilize CBT, DBT, and EMDR therapies.

3. Relational & Systemic Family Healing

An individual cannot heal in isolation while returning to an unaddressed home dynamic. We facilitate structured family psychoeducation, boundary workshops, and couples counseling to build a healthy ecosystem for sustained sobriety.

4. Purpose, Community & Aftercare

Sobriety is not just the absence of alcohol or substances; it is the presence of meaningful purpose, connection, and joy. We help residents rebuild self-worth, career direction, and an active lifelong peer network.
