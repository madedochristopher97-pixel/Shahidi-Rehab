Care with dignity, close to home.

Restora Centre for Recovery treats addiction and the mental health struggles that often come with it. We combine medical care, therapy and family support in a calm setting in Nairobi.

What we believe

Addiction is a health condition

It is not a lack of willpower. With proper medical and emotional support, recovery is possible.

Dignity comes first

No shaming, no pressure and no judgement. You decide your pace, with our guidance.

Families heal together

Relationships are part of recovery, so we involve the people who matter to you, with your consent.

Your care team

Several specialists work together on one plan for you.

Doctors and psychiatrists

Assess you at admission and oversee detox and medication.

Nurses

Monitor your health and comfort throughout your stay.

Psychologists and counsellors

Lead one-to-one and group therapy.

Family and aftercare coordinators

Involve your loved ones and plan life after discharge.

The centre

Healing is easier in a calm place. Our centre is on Old Namanga Road, Nairobi, with quiet gardens and private rooms.

Have questions about how we can help?

Speak to our admissions team. We will listen, explain our programs and walk you through each step.
