Care with dignity, close to home.

Open 24 hours a day, every day. Restora Centre for Recovery treats addiction and the mental health struggles that often come with it. We combine medical care, therapy and family support in a calm setting in Kajiado county.

Our vision

Lives renewed, potential rediscovered

To be a leading centre of excellence in holistic wellness and rehabilitation, where lives are renewed, potential is rediscovered and every individual is empowered to embrace a healthier, purposeful and fulfilling future.

Our mission

Care that is safe, supportive and dignified

To provide compassionate, professional and holistic wellness and rehabilitation services in a safe, supportive and dignified environment. Through evidence-informed care, counselling and personalised treatment, we empower individuals to overcome life’s challenges.

Our philosophy

Every person has a story. Every story deserves hope.

Our core values

Compassion

We meet every individual with empathy and kindness.

Dignity and respect

We honour the worth and uniqueness of every person.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, accountability and professionalism.

Hope

We believe in positive change and new beginnings.

Transformation

We support personal growth and lasting recovery.

Empowerment

We equip individuals with the skills and confidence to thrive.

What we believe

Addiction is a health condition

It is not a lack of willpower. With proper medical and emotional support, recovery is possible.

Dignity comes first

No shaming, no pressure and no judgement. You decide your pace, with our guidance.

Families heal together

Relationships are part of recovery, so we involve the people who matter to you, with your consent.

Your care team

Several specialists work together on one plan for you.

Doctors and psychiatrists

Assess you at admission and oversee detox and medication.

Nurses

Monitor your health and comfort throughout your stay.

Psychologists and counsellors

Lead one-to-one and group therapy.

Family and aftercare coordinators

Involve your loved ones and plan life after discharge.

The centre
