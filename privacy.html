Data Protection & Ethics

Privacy & Medical Confidentiality Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Our Non-Negotiable Confidentiality Pledge

At Shahidi Centre for Recovery, confidentiality is the cornerstone of our clinical practice. We understand that seeking addiction treatment requires immense vulnerability. All inquiries, clinical evaluations, and resident records are protected under medical privacy laws and the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information solely to provide safe medical and psychotherapeutic care. This includes contact details provided voluntarily during inquiry, medical history, psychiatric assessments, and emergency contact details. We never sell, rent, or trade your data.

3. Communications and Marketing

We do not use phone numbers or emails collected during intake for commercial marketing or unsolicited campaigns. Contact information is strictly used for direct care coordination and authorized family updates.

4. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding personal data privacy, email us at privacy@shahidirecovery.co.ke or call our administration desk at +254 712 345 678.
