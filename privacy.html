Privacy and confidentiality

Last updated: October 2026

Our promise

Confidentiality is central to our care. Enquiries, assessments and treatment records are protected under medical privacy rules and the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

Only what we need to care for you: contact details you give us, medical and psychological history, and emergency contacts. We never sell, rent or trade your data.

How we use your contact details

To coordinate your care and, with your consent, to update your family. We do not use your details for marketing.

Enquiry forms

Our forms open your email app so you can send us a message. Nothing is stored on this website.
