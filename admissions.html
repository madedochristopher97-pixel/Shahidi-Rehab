Here's Exactly What Happens When You Reach Out

Reaching out for help is often accompanied by apprehension. We want you to feel completely informed and at ease. You will not face sales pressure or invasive interrogations—only an attentive listener who cares about your safety.

The 4-Step Process

From Your First Call to Settling In

Every step of our intake is designed to remove friction, protect your confidentiality, and ensure that medical and emotional needs are prioritized.

1

Confidential Initial Call or Message

When you call or WhatsApp our helpline, you speak directly with an experienced clinical intake counselor. In this unhurried conversation, we listen to your situation, answer initial questions, and discuss whether Shahidi is the right clinical fit for your needs. There is no obligation to commit.

2

Comprehensive Clinical & Medical Assessment

Our Medical Director and psychological team conduct a confidential pre-admission screening. We evaluate current substance use patterns, medical history, physical symptoms, and any co-occurring anxiety or mood conditions to draft your individualized detox and therapy roadmap.

3

Transparent Financial Planning & Travel Arrangements

We review program costs clearly with zero surprise fees. Our team coordinates with your private insurance provider where applicable or outlines transparent private-pay schedules. For residents arriving from outside Nairobi or abroad, we coordinate discreet airport pickup and transport.

4

Warm Arrival & Compassionate Intake

Upon arriving at our Karen sanctuary, you are welcomed with warmth and hot tea. Our nursing staff conducts a gentle medical check-in, shows you to your private room, and allows you time to rest and orient yourself before beginning your treatment plan.

Plain Language Finance

Insurance, Payment & Financial Transparency

We believe in honest, straightforward communication about treatment costs. We will never overpromise on insurance coverage or leave you with unexpected bills after treatment.

  • Private Insurance Verification: We work with leading Kenyan and international health insurance providers. In our initial consultation, we verify benefits confidentially on your behalf.
  • All-Inclusive Residential Rates: Our residential fees cover your private room, 24/7 medical oversight, doctor visits, individual therapy, group counseling, chef-prepared meals, and facility amenities.
  • Flexible Self-Pay Options: For families self-funding care, we structure transparent phased payment arrangements.

Preparation Guide

Preparing for Your Stay: What to Bring

Our sanctuary provides all linens, personal towels, nutritious meals, and clinical supplies. Here is a practical guide to help you pack comfortably.

What to Bring

  • 7–10 days of comfortable, casual clothing (wash facilities available)
  • Walking shoes/sneakers and light workout gear for garden strolls and yoga
  • Valid National ID or Passport
  • Health insurance cards (if applicable)
  • Current prescribed medications in their original pharmacy bottles
  • Personal journal, favourite books, or reading material

What to Leave at Home

  • Alcohol, non-prescribed substances, or unsealed medications
  • Alcohol-based perfumes, colognes, or mouthwashes
  • Weapons or sharp objects of any kind
  • Excessive jewelry, high cash amounts, or irreplaceable valuables
  • Work laptops during the initial 7-day stabilization phase (stored securely in centre safe)

Communication & Visitors

  • During the first 7 days, residents disconnect from outside digital distractions to settle their nervous systems
  • Designated phone check-in hours with loved ones occur twice weekly thereafter
  • Weekend visiting hours are warmly welcomed following clinical stabilization
  • Family therapy sessions can be scheduled on-site or via secure video link
