Four steps to admission

  1. Call or message us

    You will speak with a trained intake counsellor. Ask anything you like. There is no obligation to continue.

  2. Medical and clinical assessment

    Our clinical team talks through your health, substance use and mental wellbeing, then plans your care with you.

  3. Costs and arrangements

    We explain fees clearly, work with your insurer where possible, and help arrange transport if you are travelling.

  4. Arrival

    Our nurses welcome you, do a gentle health check and show you to your room. You can rest before treatment begins.

Costs, explained plainly

We tell you the full cost before you commit. There are no surprise charges.

  • Insurance. We check your cover confidentially and tell you what it pays for.
  • Residential fees cover your room, medical care, therapy and meals.
  • Paying privately? Payment plans are available.

What to bring

We provide bedding, towels, meals and clinical supplies.

Bring

  • 7 to 10 days of comfortable clothes
  • Walking shoes and light exercise wear
  • National ID or passport
  • Insurance card, if you have one
  • Prescribed medication in its original packaging
  • A journal or books

Leave at home

  • Alcohol and non-prescribed substances
  • Alcohol-based perfume or mouthwash
  • Weapons or sharp objects
  • Valuables and large amounts of cash
  • Work laptops for the first 7 days

Calls and visits

  • For the first 7 days, you rest without outside contact
  • After that, set phone times with loved ones twice a week
  • Weekend visits are welcome once you are settled
  • Family sessions can be held on site or by video

Admissions questions

Can I be admitted the same day?

Often, yes. If a bed is free and you are medically cleared, same-day admission is possible. Call us as early as you can.

What if my loved one refuses help?

This is common. It is usually fear or shame. We can coach you on how to talk with them without blame, and how to hold firm, loving boundaries.

Are rooms private or shared?

Most residents have a private room. A limited number of shared rooms are available for people who prefer company.
