What happens when you reach out.

No pressure and no judgement. Our team is open 24 hours a day. Here are the three steps, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Three steps to admission

  1. Call or message us

    You will speak with a trained intake counsellor. Ask anything you like. There is no obligation to continue.

  2. Medical and clinical assessment

    Our clinical team talks through your health, substance use and mental wellbeing, then plans your care with you.

  3. Arrival

    Our nurses welcome you, do a gentle health check and show you to your room. You can rest before treatment begins.

Costs, explained plainly

We tell you the full cost before you commit. There are no surprise charges.

  • One clear figure. We explain the cost plainly during your admissions conversation.
  • Residential fees cover your bed, medical care, therapy and meals.
  • Ask us anything about cost before you decide. There is no obligation to continue.

What to pack

We provide bedding, towels, meals and all clinical supplies. Bring enough for 7 to 10 days.

Documents and medication

  • Photo ID, the original and a copy
  • A written list of your current medication and prescriptions
  • Prescribed medication in its original packaging. Check with us first before bringing any personal medicine

Clothing, 7 to 10 days

  • Comfortable clothes: t-shirts, sweatpants and shorts
  • Socks and underwear
  • Sleepwear
  • Trainers or walking shoes with non-slip soles

Toiletries and personal aids

  • Toothbrush, toothpaste and soap, unopened and alcohol-free
  • A hairbrush or comb, and shaving supplies
  • Glasses, contact lenses or hearing aids

Not allowed at the centre

Electronic devices are not permitted. That includes phones, laptops and tablets. It is a clinical decision rather than a punishment: it protects your focus and everyone else’s privacy. Also leave behind outside food, and anything containing alcohol, including perfume and mouthwash.

Calls and visits

Staying connected to family is part of treatment, not a break from it.

  • Family can visit on scheduled visiting days. Visits are an important part of our family program.
  • Some restrictions apply early on, during detox and stabilisation, so that you can rest and settle.
  • The clinical team explains the schedule on admission and updates it as treatment progresses.
  • Family sessions can be held on site, with your consent.

Admissions questions

Can I be admitted the same day?

Often, yes. If a bed is free and you are medically cleared, same-day admission is possible. Call us as early as you can.

What if my loved one refuses help?

This is common. It is usually fear or shame. We can coach you on how to talk with them without blame, and how to hold firm, loving boundaries.

Are rooms private or shared?

Rooms are shared. We do not offer private rooms. Sharing is part of how residential treatment works: living alongside others in recovery is one of the things that makes it effective.

How quickly can I admit a loved one?

In most cases, within 24 hours of the first call. For urgent situations, after a medical emergency or a relapse, same-day admission is usually possible. The faster the admission, the more you will need to bring without preparation, but our team will talk you through what is essential.

Can I tour the centre before admitting?

Yes. We offer in-person tours for families considering admission. Tours are by appointment, so that we can give you our attention and you can meet members of the clinical team. Call us to arrange a time.

Can someone be admitted against their will?

Involuntary admission is possible in specific circumstances under Kenyan law, typically where the client poses a clear and immediate risk to themselves or others and a medical professional supports the decision. We treat these admissions carefully and always work towards the client consenting to continued treatment as soon as it is clinically possible. Call us to discuss your situation.
