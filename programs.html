Treatment Is a Journey, Not a Menu

True recovery is not a rigid list of disconnected services. It is a seamless clinical journey tailored to your unique biology, history, and goals—progressing safely from initial medical detox to deep residential healing, social re-entry, and enduring aftercare.

Level 1 · Physical Stabilization

Physician-Monitored Medical Detox

Withdrawal from alcohol, opioids, or benzodiazepines can feel physically and mentally overwhelming when attempted alone. At Shahidi, you are never alone. Our licensed psychiatrists and nursing staff monitor your vitals, sleep, and emotional wellbeing 24/7.

Who it's for: Individuals experiencing physical dependency, acute alcohol withdrawal, prescription medication dependence, or opioid use disorder needing immediate medical clearance.

Therapies used: Pharmacological stabilization, electrolyte and nutrient repletion, supportive counseling, and gentle rest therapies.

What a day looks like: Regular vital checks, wholesome chef-prepared broth and light meals, soothing herbal teas, quiet private recovery room, and gentle conversations with our caring medical team.

Inquire About Detox Intake
Level 2 · Core Inpatient Sanctuary

Comprehensive Residential Inpatient Program

Residential care is where deep, foundational healing takes place. With standard 30, 60, or 90-day stays, you reside in a serene, private room with access to our peaceful garden grounds, gym, meditation hall, and organic dining.

Who it's for: Individuals who need a dedicated, supportive environment away from toxic social circles, high-stress triggers, or access to substances to break chronic relapse cycles.

Therapies used: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), somatic nervous system regulation, expressive art therapy, and dual-diagnosis psychiatric care.

What a day looks like: Gentle morning mindfulness and yoga, farm-fresh breakfast, one-on-one psychotherapy, group psychoeducation, garden walking, fitness, and evening community reflection.

Level 3 · Flexible Community Transition

Outpatient & Intensive Outpatient (IOP)

Recovery must translate into the real world. Our Outpatient programs offer evidence-based clinical therapy with the flexibility to sleep at home, continue work, or attend school.

Who it's for: Graduates of our residential program transitioning home, or individuals with moderate substance misuse and strong home stability who cannot take extended leave from work.

Structure: 3 to 5 sessions weekly (morning or evening tracks), combining individual psychotherapy, peer group dynamic workshops, and family check-ins.

Level 4 · Lifelong Freedom

Continuing Care & Alumni Fellowship

The day you graduate from Shahidi is just the beginning of a lifelong sisterhood and brotherhood. Our aftercare network provides structure, accountability, and genuine fellowship for the years ahead.

Includes: Weekly moderated alumni recovery circles, one-on-one check-ins with your primary therapist, sober social events, emergency trigger hotlines, and peer mentorship.

Relapse response: If a slip or challenge occurs, we respond with immediate empathy and rapid recalibration, never punitive judgment or abandonment.

Whole-Family Recovery

Because Addiction Impacts the Entire Family

When an individual battles addiction, parents, partners, siblings, and children often carry silent wounds: chronic anxiety, broken trust, guilt, and emotional exhaustion.

Our dedicated Family Program is designed to heal the collective family system. We guide loved ones through educational workshops that explain the science of addiction, teach healthy boundary-setting, and facilitate safe, facilitated reconciliation conversations.

  • Weekend Family Workshops: Compassionate psychoeducation explaining neurobiology and dismantling guilt.
  • Facilitated Reconciliation Sessions: Safe dialogue with your loved one guided by certified family therapists.
  • Home Transition Planning: Concrete strategies to prepare a peaceful, supportive sober living environment.
