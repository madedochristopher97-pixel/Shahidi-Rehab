Patient rights and responsibilities

Last updated: November 2026

Recovery works best when everyone knows what to expect. These are the rights every person in our care has, and the responsibilities we ask each person to share so the centre stays safe and respectful for all.

Your rights

  • Dignity and respect. To be treated with courtesy and without shame, judgement or discrimination, whatever your background, beliefs or history.
  • Voluntary treatment. Treatment at Restora is voluntary. You can ask questions, say no to a proposed treatment, or decide to leave. If you choose to leave against medical advice, our team will explain the risks and help you leave as safely as possible.
  • Information and consent. To have your assessment, treatment plan and any medicine explained in plain language, and to give or withhold your consent before treatment begins.
  • Clear costs. To have the full cost of your care explained before you commit to anything.
  • Privacy and confidentiality. To have your records and personal information protected, as set out in our confidentiality statement and privacy policy.
  • Family involvement on your terms. To decide whether, and how, your family is involved in your care.
  • Safe care. To receive care from qualified staff, in a clean and safe environment free from violence, abuse and harassment.
  • Your beliefs. To have your culture, faith and personal values respected in your care.
  • Raising concerns. To make a complaint or give feedback without it affecting your care. See our complaints and feedback policy.

Your responsibilities

  • Be open with us. Give our team honest and complete information about your health, substance use and medicines, so your care is safe.
  • Tell us how you feel. Let staff know straight away if your symptoms change or you feel unwell or unsafe.
  • Take part. Join in your treatment plan, therapy and group sessions as agreed with your team.
  • Keep the centre safe. Violence, weapons, non-prescribed substances and harassment are not allowed, as set out in our terms.
  • Respect other residents. Treat others with the same dignity you expect, and keep what you learn about other people in treatment private.
  • Follow centre arrangements. Respect visiting arrangements, which are by appointment to protect everyone’s privacy, and look after the centre’s property.
  • Agreed fees. Settle fees as agreed with our admissions team, and talk to us early if anything changes.
