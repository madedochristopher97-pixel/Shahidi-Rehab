Admissions Charter

Terms & Admissions Conditions

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Voluntary & Dignified Treatment

Admission to Shahidi Centre for Recovery is voluntary and grounded in mutual respect between resident, family, and clinical team. We provide a therapeutic community that promotes autonomy, safety, and personal responsibility.

2. Community Safety Guidelines

To preserve our sanctuary as a safe haven for all residents, physical violence, weapons, contraband substances, or harassment of any kind are strictly prohibited on grounds.

3. Medical Decisions and Protocol

All medical detoxification and pharmacotherapy regimens are determined exclusively by licensed physicians following individualized clinical examinations in accordance with Ministry of Health guidelines.

4. Questions & Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding our admissions agreement, please speak with our admissions desk at care@shahidirecovery.co.ke or call +254 712 345 678.
