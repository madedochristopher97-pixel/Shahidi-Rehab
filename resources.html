For Families & Caregivers

Walking Alongside Someone You Love

If someone close to you is battling substance dependency, you may feel overwhelmed by conflicting emotions: love, grief, anger, and panic. You do not have to carry this alone.

Our family advocacy team provides confidential consultations for spouses, parents, and adult children. We help you evaluate the severity of the situation, guide you in staging a gentle intervention if necessary, and connect you with family support groups.

  • Confidential Family Guidance: Advice tailored to your unique family dynamics and history.
  • Intervention Support: Clinical mediation to help a resistant loved one accept inpatient care willingly.
  • Family Psychoeducation: Learning how to support recovery without unintentionally enabling addiction.
