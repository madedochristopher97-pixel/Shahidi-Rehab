Emergency notice

Last updated: November 2026

If someone is in danger right now, call 999 or 112, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Do not wait, and do not drive yourself if you are unwell.

Restora is not an emergency service

Our phone line is open 24 hours a day for advice, admissions and support, but we cannot send an ambulance or give emergency medical treatment over the phone or through this website.

Call for help straight away if someone

  • has taken too much alcohol or drugs and cannot be woken, or is breathing very slowly, noisily or not at all
  • has blue or grey lips or fingertips, or very small pupils after using opioids
  • is having a seizure (fit)
  • is very confused, very agitated, or seeing or hearing things that are not there, especially after stopping alcohol
  • has chest pain, a very high temperature or a severe headache
  • has hurt themselves, or is talking about ending their life

While you wait for help

  • Stay with the person and keep them as calm as you can.
  • If they are unconscious but breathing, lay them on their side so they do not choke.
  • Do not give them more alcohol, drugs or anything to eat or drink.
  • If you can, tell the emergency team what they took, how much, and when.

Other free support in Kenya

  • NACADA helpline, 1192. Free counselling and referral for alcohol and drug problems.
  • Kenya Red Cross, 1199. Free toll-free line, including counselling and psychosocial support.

When it is not an emergency

Once everyone is safe, call us on +254 795 180 704, any time of day or night. We can talk through what happened and the next steps, including medically supervised detox.
